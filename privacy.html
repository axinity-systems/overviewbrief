Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OverviewBrief ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit our website at overviewbrief.netlify.app (the "Site").

Information We May Collect

OverviewBrief is a static informational and media website. We do not intentionally collect personal information through user accounts, login systems, or form submissions because the Site does not include these features.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand aggregate website traffic and usage patterns. These services may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This information is used to improve the Site and understand what content our visitors find valuable.

If analytics services are active on the Site, they operate through their own data collection mechanisms. Please refer to the privacy policies of the respective analytics providers for more information about their data practices.

Social Embeds

Our AI News section may contain embedded content from Instagram. When you visit pages with Instagram embeds, your browser may make requests to Instagram/Meta servers. Instagram/Meta may process information according to their own privacy policies, which are available on their respective websites.

Embedded content from other websites behaves in the same way as if you visited the other website directly. These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with the embedded content.

External Links

The Site contains links to external websites, including Instagram, X (Twitter), and other third-party services. These websites are outside our direct control. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you visit.

Cookies

The Site itself does not intentionally use unnecessary tracking cookies. However, third-party services and embedded content (such as Instagram embeds) may use their own cookies and similar technologies as part of their normal operation. We do not have direct control over these third-party technologies.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Our content is general-audience and focused on technology education and news. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Site, please contact us and we will work to address the situation.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Site's functionality changes or as our practices evolve. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to periodically review this policy.
