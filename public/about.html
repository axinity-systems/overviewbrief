About OverviewBrief

OverviewBrief is an AI media and education platform focused on making artificial intelligence easier to understand and keeping people informed about the AI developments that actually matter.

There's a lot of noise in AI right now. Every day brings new announcements, new models, new tools, and new claims about what AI can or can't do. OverviewBrief exists to cut through that noise and focus on what's actually important and explain it in a way that makes sense.

What We Do

AI News

Current AI developments presented in a concise, accessible format. New model releases, research breakthroughs, tool launches, and industry shifts curated so you stay informed without the overwhelm.

Learn AI

Evergreen explanations of AI concepts for people who want to understand the technology without needing a technical background. From LLMs to AI agents to RAG, complicated ideas broken down into clear, approachable explanations.

Our Approach

We believe that understanding AI shouldn't require a computer science degree. The technology is becoming too important to be understood only by a small group of experts. Our goal is to bridge the gap between technical AI discourse and everyday understanding.

OverviewBrief is a place to stay informed about AI and actually understand what everyone is talking about.
